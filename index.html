Jelajahi Kami Hubungi Kami
MORE ABOUT US

Mr. Djonni Gumulya appointed his best team: Andreas K, Mrs. Veranita and Mrs. Sylvia, to establish PT. Bintang Semesta Farma. The business began in 2010 with the establishment of a pharmacy business. This initial success was then further expanded in 2013 by starting a Pharmaceutical Wholesaler Business (PBF).

Journey Begins
  • 2016 BSF was appointed by DARYA VARIA as sales point of Skin Care.
  • 2018 formed a sales team, opened depot offices in Cirebon, Bandung, Semarang and Bekasi.
  • 2019 BSF was appointed by DKT INDONESIA as its distributor.
  • 2020 BSF was appointed by DEXA CHD as its distributor.
  • 2020 opened Depot offices in Malang City.
  • 2021 opened Branch Office in Semarang City.
  • 2022 opened Branch Office in Sidoarjo City.
  • 2023 opened Branch Office in Bandung City.
